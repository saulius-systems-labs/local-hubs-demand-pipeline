# Local Hubs Demand Pipeline

An early-stage project exploring how local, evidence-led hubs can help small and medium-sized businesses identify practical workflow improvements and connect with relevant learning or implementation support.

## Proposed focus

- Start with discovery in Vilnius; explore Kaunas and London through low-cost research and expressions of interest.
- Publish useful local evidence and resources, not thin or duplicated city pages.
- Explore three visitor needs: **Learn** (build skills), **Build** (prototype a workflow), and **Improve** (assess an existing process).
- Treat any physical venue, community organization, or delivery partner as prospective until a relationship and its permitted description are confirmed in writing.

## Initial workstreams

1. Define audience, consent language, data minimization, retention, and human-review responsibilities before collecting leads.
2. Validate local evidence and CMS fields; exclude records without authoritative source links and verification.
3. Design a small, genuinely useful directory pilot with original local information and accessibility/responsive checks.
4. Prototype intake only in a non-production environment. No automated customer messages or production data access are in scope for this discovery phase.
5. Review results and agree any public launch, partner references, budget, and compensation separately before making commitments.

## Status and guardrails

This is a proposal for discussion, not a claim that named venues have agreed to participate or that a service is live. No partner endorsement, address/phone details, pricing, payment promise, revenue allocation, credits, or implementation commitment is published here. Those details require verification and explicit approval before publication. Do not add personal lead data, credentials, production keys, or customer records to this repository.

## How to contribute

Open a discussion with your city, the SME workflow problem you see, what evidence would make a local resource trustworthy, and a small pilot idea. Please do not include personal or confidential information.
